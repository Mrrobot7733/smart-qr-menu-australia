4. Fees, Volume Tiers & Price Structure Evolution

4.1 Fixed Rate Guarantee: The agreed Monthly Subscription Fee ($99.00 AUD/mo) is guaranteed and locked for the duration of the Initial 12-Month Term.
4.2 Baseline Volume Cap: The introductory flat monthly fee applies to venues processing up to $25,000.00 AUD in Gross Monthly Order Volume ("GMV") through the platform per calendar month.
4.3 High-Volume Tiers & Fee Adjustments (Right to Introduce Volume Fees): Following the expiration of the Initial Term, or in the event the Client GMV exceeds $25,000.00 AUD/month on a continuous basis, the Provider reserves the right, upon providing thirty (30) days written notice, to:

  • Transition the venue to the appropriate Volume Tier (e.g., Pro Tier at $149.00 AUD/mo for up to $60k/mo GMV); or
  • Apply a nominal technology volume fee (not to exceed 0.75%) exclusively on the incremental GMV exceeding the baseline $25,000.00 AUD threshold.
